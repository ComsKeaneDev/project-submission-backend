Sponsorship

About Access Bristol

Access Bristol is an initiative within the University of Bristol dedicated to developing assistive technology solutions through equal partnership between people with disabilities, students, researchers, and industry partners.

We are not a separate legal entity. We operate as a university initiative using Bristol’s infrastructure, which allows us to receive and direct funding efficiently without administrative overhead.

Our Approach

Access Bristol runs proposal development events where diverse teams create grant-ready project plans for assistive technology solutions. Unlike traditional hackathons focused on rapid prototyping, we emphasize sustained engagement and deep collaboration that leads to meaningful, long-term projects.

Selected projects receive up to 12 months of support, including:

  • Volunteer mentorship from technical and managerial experts
  • Project management guidance
  • Resource coordination
  • Connection to funding opportunities

How to Volunteer

We need volunteers at two stages:

During Events (2 days)

  • Help teams develop comprehensive project plans
  • Support resource scoping and feasibility assessment
  • Facilitate connections between participants

Ongoing Mentorship (flexible commitment)

  • Provide technical or managerial guidance to selected projects
  • Offer advisement at whatever level fits your availability
  • Share expertise from your professional experience

Volunteer Options:

  • Individual volunteers engaging outside work hours
  • Company-sponsored volunteers with employer permission to volunteer during work time

Sponsorship Opportunities

What We Need Funding For:

The University of Bristol provides event space, insurance, and substantial staff time. Sponsorship covers:

  • Food and refreshments for all participants
  • Project supplies and prototyping materials
  • Access stipends to cover additional participation costs for people with disabilities (examples: sighted guides for blind participants in unfamiliar environments, transportation assistance, accommodation needs)
  • Event materials and documentation

What Sponsors Receive:

Volunteer-Based Sponsorship:
Companies that provide employee volunteer hours receive:

  • Logo placement on event materials and website
  • Recognition in event communications

Financial or Enhanced Support:
Additional volunteer hours or fiscal contributions can include:

  • Expanded marketing presence
  • Ability to propose specific thematic focus areas for projects
  • Customized partnership terms (we’re flexible and open to discussion)

Partnership Terms

We maintain a dedicated university account that:

  • Receives funds directly without overhead costs
  • Allows sponsors to specify how their contribution is used
  • Provides university accountability and transparency

All sponsorship terms are negotiable. We’re interested in partnerships that align with our commitment to equal participation and sustained engagement in assistive technology development.
